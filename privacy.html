Privacy policy

game version 2.9.1 · policy version 2 · in force from 18 November 2026

The short version

  • Challengers collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting service.
  • Playing with a friend nearby uses Bluetooth and needs no internet. Playing online (the ONLINE button) uses the internet, through a small relay server that only passes the match from one phone to the other.
  • Online play sends your controls and the match settings and nothing about you: no name, no account, no contacts, no location. Nothing is stored on a server.
  • The only things it saves are your settings, your gun levels and your match history, on your own phone.
  • You can delete everything it saved: Settings > General > My data > DELETE ALL.

the Challengers project, run by an individual developer (not a company). You can write to mohamedyounis248@gmail.com.

What the game saves on your phone

  • Your settings: language, volume, vibration, stick style and size, where your control buttons are, how you aim and dash, the camera, the match length, your chosen gun and arena, and the training-bot choices.
  • A random 4-digit match code the game makes up the first time you host a match. It is not connected to you in any way.
  • Which patch notes you have read.
  • Your Game History: for each match, when it ended, the arena, the two guns, who won, the rounds and kills, how long it lasted, and how many shots, Supers, dashes and walls you used. There are no names and nothing that identifies the other player.
  • Your gun levels: how much experience each of the five guns has earned from your matches, and so the level (0 to 10) of each gun. Nothing in it identifies you.

All of this lives in the game's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it, and the game does not send it anywhere. (The one exception is the match itself: when you play online, the match settings, including the level of your gun, go to your opponent through the relay: see Online play.)

To remove it: use DELETE ALL in the game's settings, or Android Settings > Apps > Challengers > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall the game.

A note about Android itself: if you have Android's backup switched on, Android may copy an app's saved files to your Google account under Google's own rules. The game does not do this and cannot control it.

Bluetooth and nearby devices

To play with a friend nearby the two phones talk directly to each other over Bluetooth Low Energy. Over Bluetooth nothing goes through the internet or a server.

  • When you host, your phone announces a short tag so your friend's phone can find it: the game's own service identifier, the game version and the 4-digit match code. It does not announce your name, your phone's Bluetooth name or anything else about you. (Test builds before 2.8.2 also announced the phone's Bluetooth name while hosting. That was removed.)
  • Anyone nearby who has the game can see that a match is being hosted. The code decides which phone a friend joins; it is not a password. The hosting phone accepts only one connected phone.
  • During a match the phones exchange the match settings (arena, guns, the level of each gun, score limit, round length and a random number), each player's controls (movement, aim and buttons) and checksums that detect when the two games disagree. Nothing from this exchange is stored.
  • The game does not add its own encryption to this exchange. Someone with Bluetooth sniffing equipment inside radio range could read the controls and the match settings. They contain no personal data.

Online play (the ONLINE button)

ONLINE connects your phone to a small server, the relay, which the developer runs on Cloudflare Workers. The relay passes each phone's game messages to the other phone. The two phones never connect to each other directly, so your opponent never sees your internet address; the relay (that is, Cloudflare, which runs it) does.

What is sent:

  • the match settings: the arena, the two guns, the level of each gun, the score limit, the round length and a random number;
  • your controls (movement, aim and buttons) and checksums that detect when the two games disagree;
  • the game's version number, so that only phones on the same version are paired;
  • for a QUICK MATCH, the level of your gun, so that a player of a similar level can be found;
  • for a friend's room, a 6-digit room code that the game makes up when you create the room.

What is not sent: your name, an account, your e-mail or phone number, contacts, location, the advertising ID or any identifier of your phone. There is no chat and no voice.

What is kept: nothing. A room exists in the relay's memory while the two phones are connected (for at most 45 minutes) and is forgotten when they leave. The relay does not read, store or log the game messages, and the developer keeps no record of who played or what was sent. Like any internet service, Cloudflare sees the internet address of each phone that connects and may keep network logs under Cloudflare's own privacy policy; the developer does not collect internet addresses.

The connection to the relay is encrypted (TLS).

Please know: anyone who knows a 6-digit room code can try to join a room that is waiting for its second player, so share the code only with the friend you want to play. A room takes only two players. In a quick match the other player's gun level is what their phone says; the game cannot check that it is real.

What the game does not do

  • It uses the internet only for online play (and when you tap PRIVACY or TERMS, to open these pages in your browser). Bluetooth matches, Practice and everything else never use it.
  • It has no accounts and no sign-in, and no chat or way for players to message each other.
  • It shows no ads and uses no advertising ID.
  • It has no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting service.
  • It does not read your location (see the table above), camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or phone number.

These web pages

The game's settings open this page and the Terms of use in your phone's browser. They are plain pages hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, as the host, may record that your address asked for a page, under GitHub's own privacy statement. The pages load no scripts, fonts, images or trackers from anywhere else. The game itself uses the internet only for online play, as described above.

Children

Challengers is not designed for children under 13. It collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Your choices

You can delete everything the game saved at any time (see above). Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing on the developer's side to look up, correct or delete. If you have a question, write to mohamedyounis248@gmail.com.
