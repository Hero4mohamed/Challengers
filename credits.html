Credits

game version 2.8.3

Engine

Made with Unity (Unity Technologies).

Fonts

Tajawal (Regular, Bold, ExtraBold) by Boutros International, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1: github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/tajawal. One typeface for Latin and Arabic.

Sound effects

  • Gunshots: recordings from "Gunshot Sounds" by Tabasco (opengameart.org/content/gunshot-sounds), credited to Vincent Sevedge / Tabasco under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 licence (creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0). Changes made: cut into single shots, mixed to mono, faded at the edges and level-matched.
  • Impact, explosion and interface sounds: by Kenney (kenney.nl), "Impact Sounds", "Sci-fi Sounds" and "Interface Sounds", Creative Commons Zero (CC0).
  • Every other sound (dash, walls, reloads, round jingles, shield, medkit, the Gates and the rocket) was synthesised by the developer for this game.

Art, arenas and code

The five robots, the arenas, the shaders, the interface and all the game code were made for this project. There is no third-party art.

Logo and app icon

The Challengers logo and app icon were generated with ChatGPT (OpenAI) from the developer's description, then chosen and directed by the developer.

Bluetooth

The game talks to other phones through Android's standard Bluetooth Low Energy interfaces.
