Challengers

game version 2.8.3

A two-player robot arena game for Android phones. It plays over Bluetooth between two phones in the same room: no internet, no accounts, no ads.

Five guns, five arenas, a training bot for playing alone, and a Super that changes how your gun fires.

Android 8 or newer with Bluetooth Low Energy. Multiplayer needs two phones nearby; Practice needs only one.
